# Video Game Playthrough Podcasts

> **A note on format:** True *video* podcasts featuring actual gameplay footage as their primary medium are rare in the RSS/Apple Podcasts ecosystem — that format lives almost entirely on YouTube and Twitch. What the podcast world offers instead is a rich category of **play-along podcasts**: audio shows (many with companion video on YouTube) structured around hosts playing a game in full, episode by episode, intended for listeners to play alongside. Some offer full video episodes via Patreon or YouTube. Both types are listed here with their format clearly noted.

## ⚠️ Why True Video Game Playthrough *Video* Podcasts Are Rare

The traditional podcast format (RSS + audio/video file per episode) is poorly suited to gameplay footage because:

1. **File size** — A 4-hour playthrough session encoded as video is several gigabytes; RSS podcast files are typically under 100 MB.
2. **Discovery** — YouTube's recommendation algorithm is far more effective for this content than podcast directories.
3. **Monetisation** — YouTube AdSense, Super Chats, and memberships outperform podcast sponsorships for long-form video content.

As a result, the vast majority of full video game playthroughs exist as **YouTube Let's Plays**, not RSS podcast video episodes. The podcasts above represent the closest equivalent in the podcast ecosystem — structured, episode-by-episode coverage designed for play-along listening.
