StreamVault

StreamVault is a browser-based IPTV dashboard powered by kptv-fast, an open-source streaming aggregator by kpirnie. It pulls live TV from 20+ free services including Pluto TV, Plex, Samsung TV Plus, Tubi, Xumo, DistroTV, LG Channels, Stirr, Vizio, Roku, LocalNow, TCL, Fire TV, Xiaomi, Tablo, Whale TV+, and more.


All connections proxied through killcors.com for cross-origin access.
